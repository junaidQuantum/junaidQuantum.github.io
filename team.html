Principal Investigator

Dr. Junaid ur Rehman

Assistant Professor, Department of Electrical Engineering

Junaid ur Rehman received his B.S. in Electrical Engineering from the National University of Sciences and Technology (NUST), Islamabad, in 2013, and his Ph.D. in Electronic Engineering from Kyung Hee University (KHU), Korea, in 2019. He held postdoctoral and research positions with groups at KHU, the Korea Institute of Science and Technology (KIST), and the SIGCOM group at the University of Luxembourg before joining KFUPM. His research spans quantum communications, quantum computing, and quantum sensing.
