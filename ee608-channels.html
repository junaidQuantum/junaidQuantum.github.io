1. Build the channel

The channels act one after another, from top to bottom: \(\mathcal N = \mathcal N_k \circ \dots \circ \mathcal N_1\). Each one is given by Kraus operators with \(\mathcal N_i(\rho) = \sum_l V_l\,\rho\,V_l^\dagger\) and \(\sum_l V_l^\dagger V_l = I\).

Exercises

  1. Load the three Lecture 8 examples one at a time and describe how the ball is deformed. Which of them keep the center fixed, and what does that say about \(\mathcal N(\pi)\)?
  2. For amplitude damping, which state does not move? What happens to every input at \(\gamma = 1\)?
  3. Load the two fiber segments. Show that together they act as amplitude damping with \(1 - \gamma = (1-\gamma_1)(1-\gamma_2)\) by comparing \(T\) and \(\vec t\) with a single segment.
  4. Try to choose \(\rho\) and \(\sigma\) so that their trace distance grows after the channel. Explain why you cannot (Lecture 9).
  5. For the dephasing channel, find \(F_{\min}\) and the inputs where it is reached. Check that the fidelity for the BB84 states equals \(1 - Q\) with \(Q = p/2\).
  6. Set a phase flip with \(p = \tfrac12\). Show by hand that the Kraus operators \(\{\ket{0}\!\bra{0}, \ket{1}\!\bra{1}\}\) give the same \(T\) and \(\vec t\) as \(\{I/\sqrt2, Z/\sqrt2\}\).
  7. Load Eve's probe. For which \(p\) does a phase flip give the same map? Relate your answer to the \(X\)-basis error \(\tfrac12\left(1 - \cos\tfrac{\theta}{2}\right)\).
