Role: PI · 2025–2026

Role: Technical Manager

Luxembourg Experimental Network for Quantum Communication Infrastructure. Goes beyond peer-to-peer quantum key distribution to build the first QKD network in Luxembourg. Funded by the European Union through the Digital Europe Programme (DEP).

Role: Team Member (Execution & Reporting)

Luxembourg Quantum Communication Infrastructure Laboratory — a national testbed for QCI enabling advanced collaborative research. Funded by the European Union–Next Generation EU, with the Department of Media, Connectivity and Digital Policy (SMC) of Luxembourg.

Quantum Enigmata

Role: Team Member (Proposal, Execution & Reporting)

Foundations for Quantum Networking with Quantum Enigmata — leveraging quantum paradoxes to develop foundations for quantum communication networks. Funded by the National Research Foundation (NRF) of Korea.

Hyperentanglement-Assisted Hybrid Quantum Communication

Role: Team Member (Proposal, Execution & Reporting)
