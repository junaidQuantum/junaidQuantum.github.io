Projects

Current and past research projects at the Quantum Nexus Lab, exploring the frontiers of quantum information sciences.

Active Projects

Reinforcement Learning for Flexible Entanglement Routing

Active
Quantum Networks Quantum Computing Quantum Communications

Developing reinforcement learning algorithms for optimal entanglement routing in quantum networks. The project aims to enable end-to-end entanglement delivery while optimizing for fidelity, rate, and resource utilization.

📅 2025 – Present 👥 Dr. Junaid ur Rehman (PI) 🔗 GitHub

Quantum MIMO Communications in Correlated Loss Channels

Ongoing
Quantum Communications Theoretical Foundations

Investigating the fundamental limits of multiple-input multiple-output (MIMO) quantum communication systems operating in correlated pure-loss channels. This work extends classical diversity and multiplexing concepts to the quantum domain.

📅 2024 – Present 👥 Dr. Junaid ur Rehman, L. Oleynik, S. Koudia 🔗 arXiv

Traffic-Aware Quantum Network Planning (TAQNet)

Active
Quantum Networks Quantum Communications

Developing a traffic-aware framework for minimum-cost quantum communication network planning. The project addresses the challenge of optimizing network resources while accommodating dynamic traffic patterns in quantum networks.

📅 2024 – Present 👥 Dr. Junaid ur Rehman, I. Maity, S. Chatzinotas 🔗 IEEE TQE

Completed Projects

Quantum Property Learning for NISQ Networks

Completed
Quantum Computing Quantum Networks

Developed universal quantum witness machines for learning properties of quantum systems in NISQ networks. This work established a framework for verifying and characterizing quantum devices without full state tomography.

📅 2024 – 2025 👥 U. Khalid, Dr. J. ur Rehman, et al. 🔗 IEEE TCOM

Quantum Anonymous Communication Protocols

Completed
Quantum Communications Quantum Networks

Pioneered quantum anonymous protocols for collision detection, private information retrieval, and notification in distributed networks. These protocols ensure privacy and anonymity in quantum communication systems.

📅 2021 – 2024 👥 A. Khan, U. Khalid, Dr. J. ur Rehman 🔗 IEEE TCOM
