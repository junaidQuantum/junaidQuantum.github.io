Biography

Junaid ur Rehman received the B.S. degree in Electrical Engineering from National University of Sciences and Technology (NUST), Islamabad, in 2013, and the Ph.D. degree in Electronic Engineering from Kyung Hee University (KHU), Korea, in 2019.

He worked as a Postdoctoral Fellow, Research Associate, and Research Scientist at KHU, KIST, and the SIGCOM group at the University of Luxembourg. Currently, he is an Assistant Professor at KFUPM, Saudi Arabia.

Dr. ur Rehman serves on TPC for IEEE ICC & Globecom, and is an Exemplary Reviewer for IEEE Wireless Communications Letters (2022).
