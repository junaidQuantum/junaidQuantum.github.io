Quantum Nexus Lab

We study the fundamentals of quantum information sciences — the performance limits of quantum communication channels and the use of quantum computing to solve practical problems in communication systems.

Our Research Meet the Team

Research Areas

Communications

Quantum Communications

Channel capacity and performance limits of quantum communication channels, and the foundations of secure quantum key distribution.

Computing

Quantum Computing

Applying quantum algorithms to problems of practical interest in communication systems.

Sensing

Quantum Sensing & Metrology

Phase and parameter estimation, and measurement strategies that approach fundamental sensitivity limits.

Theory

Theoretical Foundations

Quantum information theory, probability theory, linear algebra, and complexity theory underpinning the above.
